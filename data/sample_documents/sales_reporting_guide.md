# Sales Reporting Product Guide & Specifications

## Overview
The Sales Reporting module automates weekly, monthly, and quarterly revenue reporting for sales leaders and account executives.

## Key Features
- **Automated Pipeline Velocity**: Automatically measures stage progression times, bottlenecks, and deal slippage across territories.
- **Executive Summaries**: One-click generation of narrative executive briefs summarizing weekly pipeline movements.
- **Forecast Accuracy Modeling**: Statistical models combining historical win rates with rep sentiment to generate confidence intervals.
- **Manual Effort Elimination**: Reduces rep time spent on spreadsheet compilation by an average of 4 to 6 hours per week per rep.

## Supported Data Sources
- CRM Systems: Salesforce CRM, HubSpot CRM, Custom PostgreSQL Schemas.
- Productivity Tools: Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack alerts.
