# Product Pricing Guide & Catalog

## Subscription Tiers
Pricing is based on annual subscription per user seat, billed annually in USD.

### 1. Sales Reporting Pro (SKU: PROD-REP-01)
- Unit Price: $1,200 / user / year ($100 / user / month).
- Scope: Automated weekly reporting, pipeline velocity dashboards, CRM data sync.
- Min seats: 5 seats.

### 2. Sales Intelligence & Proposal Suite (SKU: PROD-INT-02)
- Unit Price: $2,400 / user / year ($200 / user / month).
- Scope: Includes Sales Reporting Pro + Automated proposal generation, deal health scoring, RAG-grounded pitch intelligence.
- Min seats: 10 seats.

### 3. Implementation & Enablement Package (SKU: PROD-IMP-01)
- Fixed One-time Fee: $15,000.
- Scope: Dedicated Solutions Architect, CRM integration, custom KPI setup, 2 team enablement workshops.

## Volume Discounts
- 20-49 seats: 10% discount on subscription.
- 50-99 seats: 15% discount on subscription.
- 100+ seats: 20% discount on subscription.
All pricing requires formal quote validation and expires 30 days from issuance.
