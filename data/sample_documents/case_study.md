# Customer Case Study: Apex Logistics

## Customer Background
Apex Logistics is a national freight and supply chain provider with 120 sales reps and $450M in annual revenue.

## The Challenge
Sales managers spent 15 hours each month collating manual Excel spreadsheets from fragmented CRM records. Deals were stalled without visibility, and quarterly forecast errors exceeded 28%.

## The Solution
Apex deployed SalesFlow Intelligence with the automated Sales Reporting module across all regional divisions.

## Results & Business Impact
- **Time Savings**: Recovered 5 hours per week per sales rep previously wasted on administrative spreadsheet updates.
- **Reporting Velocity**: Executive forecast reports delivered in 5 minutes rather than 3 days.
- **Revenue Acceleration**: 18% improvement in pipeline velocity within 90 days.
- **ROI**: Delivered 310% first-year ROI with payback achieved in 3.8 months.
