# Business Case & ROI Calculation Assumptions

## Standard Modeling Parameters
When constructing business cases for prospective clients:

### 1. Labor Cost Parameters
- Average sales representative fully loaded cost: $120,000 / year ($60 / hour assuming 2,000 work hours/year).
- Sales manager fully loaded cost: $160,000 / year ($80 / hour).

### 2. Efficiency Gains
- Administrative time saved by automating sales reports: 4 to 6 hours / rep / week.
- Conservative baseline assumption: 4 hours / week saved (200 hours / year / rep = $12,000 annual productivity recovery per rep).

### 3. Formulas
- Annual Savings ($) = Current Annual Reporting Labor Cost * Expected Savings Percentage (typically 25% - 40%).
- Net Benefit ($) = (Annual Savings * Time Period in Years) - (Implementation Cost + (Annual Ongoing Cost * Time Period in Years)).
- Payback Period (Years) = Implementation Cost / (Annual Savings - Annual Ongoing Cost).
- ROI (%) = (Net Benefit / Total Cost of Investment) * 100%.
