# Enterprise Implementation Guide

## Implementation Phases
Standard rollout is completed in 3 structured phases over 4 to 6 weeks:

### Phase 1: Environment Setup & CRM Integration (Weeks 1-2)
- Connector deployment and OAuth handshake with CRM system.
- Historical data schema mapping and initial pipeline sync.
- User permission provisioning and SSO integration.

### Phase 2: Reporting Configuration & AI Customization (Weeks 3-4)
- Tailoring report templates, KPIs, and executive reporting cadences.
- Calibrating custom scoring models against 12 months of historical deal records.

### Phase 3: Training & Production Go-Live (Weeks 5-6)
- Sales management training and rep enablement workshops.
- Production sign-off and executive review cadence launch.
