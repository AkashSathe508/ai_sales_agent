# SalesFlow Intelligence Platform — Product Overview

## Executive Summary
SalesFlow Intelligence is an enterprise AI-powered sales execution and revenue intelligence platform. It transforms disparate sales activities, CRM records, and pipeline interactions into real-time actionable insights, automated reporting, and guided deal progression.

## Core Capabilities
- **Automated Sales Reporting**: Real-time sales dashboards, pipeline velocity tracking, and forecast accuracy models. Eliminates manual spreadsheet reporting.
- **Deal Intelligence & Health Scoring**: Predictive deal scoring based on CRM activity recency, stakeholder engagement, and historical win rates.
- **Proposal & Presentation Automation**: Instant creation of tailored sales proposals, ROI business cases, and customer presentations.
- **Enterprise Integrations**: Native bi-directional sync with Salesforce, HubSpot, Microsoft Dynamics, and PostgreSQL data warehouses.

## System Architecture
Built on a microservices-based agentic pipeline with zero-data-retention options for regulated industries.
